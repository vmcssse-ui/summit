How to use this in revision.
  1. Daily Key Term first — one word a day keeps the vocabulary alive between lessons.
  2. Sort Sixteen after a lesson: it tests whether you can see how a topic fits together, not just the words.
  3. Concept Drop and Knock-out are for the week before an assessment: fast recall, and spotting the muddles that cost marks — Id-ul-Fitr for Id-ul-Adha, deterrence for reformation.
